01 / BURNOUT RECOVERY

Does any of this feel familiar?

You keep going because people rely on you. Rest does not seem to restore your energy. Work that once mattered now feels heavy. Boundaries are difficult to hold, and your identity may have become closely tied to being capable and dependable.

02 / BURNOUT RECOVERY

A particular understanding of finance

Having worked in accountancy and senior finance roles, I understand the pressures of reporting cycles, audits, year-end deadlines and responsibility for decisions. That experience can make it easier to discuss the realities of finance work without having to explain every detail.

03 / BURNOUT RECOVERY

The REST framework

Recognise: name what is happening and notice the pressures involved. Examine: explore values, expectations and the parts of work that no longer fit. Sustain: rebuild resources through boundaries, recovery and practical changes. Transform: reconnect with purpose and a wider sense of identity.

04 / BURNOUT RECOVERY

What we may work towards

A more sustainable pace, clearer boundaries, practical ways to manage workload, renewed perspective and a plan for what needs to change. Outcomes depend on your circumstances; coaching does not promise a cure for burnout.

05 / BURNOUT RECOVERY

A note about support

Coaching is not clinical treatment. If you need support for depression, anxiety or another health condition, I will encourage you to contact an appropriate health professional. Coaching can complement clinical care where suitable.
