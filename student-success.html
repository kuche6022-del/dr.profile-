01 / COLLEGES & UNIVERSITIES

Beyond attendance and attainment

Students are balancing academic demands with transitions, finances, relationships, confidence and decisions about the future. Coaching can provide structured space to identify barriers, reconnect with strengths and take practical steps.

02 / COLLEGES & UNIVERSITIES

Possible areas of support

One-to-one coaching around motivation, confidence, study-life balance and transitions; group workshops on strengths, resilience and career readiness; staff development in coaching conversations; and consultancy on student success or mentoring programmes.

03 / COLLEGES & UNIVERSITIES

Working with your institution

Delivery could be sessional, project-based, retained or embedded. A sensible first step is to agree the student group, referral route, boundaries, measures of progress and how the work fits alongside existing academic, wellbeing and careers services.

04 / COLLEGES & UNIVERSITIES

Relevant experience

The supplied background includes professional mentoring at the University of East London and education mentoring with Inspiring the Future. Dates, role descriptions and any formal institutional affiliations should be confirmed before launch.
