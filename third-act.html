01 / LIFE AFTER 55

The transition is more than a date

Retirement and later-life change can affect routine, identity, relationships and a sense of contribution. Financial planning matters, but it does not answer every personal question: what will give shape to my days, and what do I want to be part of now?

02 / LIFE AFTER 55

Who this work may suit

You may be considering a new direction after 55, approaching retirement while still feeling emotionally unprepared, or looking for renewed connection and purpose after leaving work.

03 / LIFE AFTER 55

The SAGE framework

Self-Discovery: identify values, strengths and what you want to carry forward. Adaptive Identity: make room for change and decide what you are ready to release. Generativity & Purpose: explore contribution, connection and meaning. Engage & Embed: turn insight into routines, relationships and practical next steps.

04 / LIFE AFTER 55

Possible coaching pathways

The Pivot (8 weeks / 6 sessions) for career reinvention; The Threshold (4 months / 10 sessions) for retirement transition; The Next Chapter (6 months / 12 sessions) for building purpose and structure after retirement. These proposed packages and details require final confirmation.

05 / LIFE AFTER 55

Clear boundaries

Coaching is not therapy and does not provide pension or regulated financial advice. Where appropriate, I will signpost you to qualified professionals and recognised guidance services.
