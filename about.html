01 / ABOUT

A career built in business

I spent more than three decades in accountancy, commercial leadership and executive roles. My experience includes financial control, commercial direction, executive leadership, running an accountancy practice and serving as a charity trustee.

02 / ABOUT

The shift towards coaching

The events of 2020 sharpened my interest in how people make sense of change, pressure and purpose. I returned to university to study applied positive psychology and coaching psychology, bringing academic learning into conversation with the realities of working life.

03 / ABOUT

How I work

I aim to be warm, honest and practical. I will listen carefully, ask questions that help you think differently and work with you to identify steps that make sense in your real life. Humour is welcome; so is taking difficult things seriously.

04 / ABOUT

Career and learning

The background supplied for this website includes senior roles at A & C Music Circuit Ltd, Gobar & Co Accountants and Mojere InvestHub, alongside mentoring and voluntary work. Qualifications and dates should be checked and approved by Grace before publication.
