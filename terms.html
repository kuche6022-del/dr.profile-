01 / TERMS

Before coaching begins

The coaching agreement should set out the service, aims, session format, fees, responsibilities, confidentiality and its limits.

02 / TERMS

Changes and cancellations

A clear cancellation and rescheduling policy should be supplied to clients before sessions are booked.

03 / TERMS

Scope of service

Coaching is not therapy, medical care, legal advice or regulated financial advice. Any referral or safeguarding arrangements should be explained in the relevant agreement.
