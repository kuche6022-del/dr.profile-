YOUR QUESTIONS

Clear answers, before you begin

What is the difference between coaching and therapy?

Coaching is generally focused on goals, choices and future action. Therapy provides clinical or therapeutic support. If your needs are outside coaching, I will encourage you to seek appropriate professional help.

What happens on the discovery call?

We talk for around 30 minutes about what is happening, what you hope might change and whether coaching is a suitable next step. It is free and there is no obligation.

Do you work online?

The brief describes online work across the UK and internationally, with in-person work in London. Current location and availability should be confirmed before launch.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on the programme and your aims. Proposed pathways in the brief range from six to twelve sessions, but the right arrangement should be agreed together.

How much does coaching cost?

Fees are currently described as available on request. You can ask for details when you enquire.

Is what I say confidential?

Confidentiality and its limits are explained before coaching begins. There may be exceptions where there is a serious concern about safety or a legal obligation to share information.

Can an organisation pay for coaching?

Yes, funded arrangements can be discussed with an employer, college, charity or other organisation.

Can a young person contact RISE directly?

Young people can enquire directly or through a professional supporting them. Appropriate consent and safeguarding arrangements are required for under-18s.
