01 / WORKPLACE WELLBEING

A people-centred business issue

Workload, control, recognition, relationships and fairness all shape how people experience work. A wellbeing offer is more credible when it connects individual support with the conditions people are working in.

02 / WORKPLACE WELLBEING

Programme areas

Staff wellbeing and burnout prevention; positive psychology coaching for staff and leaders; workshops on boundaries, recovery and sustainable performance; and carefully scoped support for children, families and carers alongside relevant clinical teams.

03 / WORKPLACE WELLBEING

A phased partnership

Foundation: understand needs, stakeholders and referral pathways. Pilot: test a focused workshop or coaching cohort. Review: consider feedback and agreed measures before deciding whether to expand. Timelines and outcomes should be tailored to each organisation.

04 / WORKPLACE WELLBEING

Professional boundaries

Programmes complement, and do not replace, medical or psychological care. Confidentiality, consent, data handling, supervision, cultural context and routes to clinical support should be agreed before delivery.
