01 / YOUNG PEOPLE

For young people

This is time set aside for you. It is not a review meeting and you do not need to arrive with everything figured out. We can talk, draw, map ideas, explore strengths or work out one manageable next step. You help shape the conversation.

02 / YOUNG PEOPLE

What coaching might help with

Building confidence, recognising strengths, exploring education or work options, preparing for independent living, making a plan and finding ways to keep going when things become difficult.

03 / YOUNG PEOPLE

The RISE framework

Relationship: build trust and a sense of safety. Identity: explore strengths, values and who you are beyond a label or circumstance. Strategy: identify realistic steps towards goals that matter to you. Embed: practise skills and routines that can continue beyond coaching.

04 / YOUNG PEOPLE

For professionals and commissioners

RISE may complement the work of Leaving Care teams, Personal Advisers, colleges and charities. Possible starting points include a small one-to-one cohort, an introductory workshop or a joint scoping session. Delivery, safeguarding responsibilities, referral routes and evaluation should be agreed before a pilot begins.

05 / YOUNG PEOPLE

Safety and confidentiality

Confidentiality and its limits must be explained clearly at the outset, including what happens if there is a concern about safety. Work with under-18s requires appropriate consent and safeguarding arrangements. Enhanced DBS status, insurance, supervision and professional-code alignment must be verified before claims are published.
