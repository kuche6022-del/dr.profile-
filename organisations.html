01 / FOR ORGANISATIONS

Who I work with

Potential partners include local authority Leaving Care teams, FE colleges, universities, apprenticeship and learning providers, charities, employers, HR and wellbeing teams, hospitals and healthcare organisations.

02 / FOR ORGANISATIONS

A straightforward way to begin

First, a scoping conversation about your aims and current provision. Next, a tailored outline with delivery options and costs. Where appropriate, we can begin with a small pilot, review what is useful and agree whether to continue.

03 / FOR ORGANISATIONS

What we agree together

The audience, referral and eligibility criteria, safeguarding responsibilities, confidentiality and data arrangements, delivery format, outcome measures and review points. This clarity helps the work sit alongside existing services rather than compete with them.
