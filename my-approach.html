01 / APPROACH

What informs the work

The supplied brief references positive psychology and PERMA, character strengths, self-determination theory, solution-focused and narrative approaches, adult development, burnout research and trauma-informed principles. These ideas are used to support reflection and practical action, not to label people.

02 / APPROACH

How coaching works

We begin with a conversation about what brings you here and whether coaching feels appropriate. If we agree to work together, we set out the focus, practical arrangements, confidentiality and its limits. Sessions are collaborative, and we review progress as we go.

03 / APPROACH

Ethics and boundaries

Coaching is not therapy, medical treatment or regulated financial advice. Where a need falls outside coaching, I will encourage appropriate professional support. Any claims about professional membership, supervision, DBS status, insurance and data-protection arrangements must be verified before publication.

04 / APPROACH

A note on evidence

Frameworks can help structure a conversation, but they are not guarantees. Your circumstances, culture, resources and priorities shape what is useful.
